# Step 7 — Decision and go/no-go gates

## Main path

Proceed with **Causal Anatomy of Bounded-State Plan–Act–Verify Agents**. Treat P0 as the experimental apparatus, not the novelty claim.

Preregistered pilot gate:

1. Learned triggering closes at least half of the oracle-trigger improvement on held-out tasks.
2. The paired replan effect has a confidence interval excluding zero in at least one prespecified failure family.
3. At matched final success, learned triggering uses no more than one quarter of periodic planner calls.
4. The conclusion survives at least two VLA backbones or executor variants.

If the first three fail, stop system-building and retain the result only as a diagnostic study. If the fourth fails, do not claim a general agentic mechanism.

## Backup path

Proceed with **Memory Is Not Thinking** only if tied latent iterations outperform equal-FLOP untied depth, temporal carry selectively helps state-aliasing tasks, and donor-memory swaps predictably alter target choice.

Target fit: CoRL 2027 for a robotics mechanism paper; NeurIPS 2027 only with multi-backbone causal identification and a reusable benchmark/protocol; RSS 2027 only with credible real-robot recovery evidence.
