                                         facing representation, so action-loss gradients pass through the fusion interface and broadly act on inherited representations. (b) In structured shaping, Action

                                         multimodal pathway. Gradients to θpath , θFuse , θAQF , and Q denote action-loss updates to the inherited pathway, interface parameters, and query states.
